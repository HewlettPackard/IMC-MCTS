# Code of conduct

This project welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Help others participate in a respectful, technically rigorous discussion.

## Expected conduct

- Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Give constructive, specific feedback and acknowledge uncertainty and mistakes.
- Respect other people's time, privacy, attribution and intellectual property.
- Make room for questions and explain technical context when it helps others contribute.
- Represent simulation results, assumptions and limitations honestly.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexual attention, personal insults, deliberate disruption and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable. Do not retaliate against someone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Scope and reporting

These expectations apply to the project's issues, pull requests, discussions and other project-managed spaces. They also apply when someone publicly represents the project.

Report a conduct concern privately to the maintainer contact listed in [CITATION.cff](CITATION.cff). Describe what happened, where it occurred and any relevant context. Do not post private information or accusations in a public issue. If a maintainer is involved, use another available project maintainer or the relevant platform's reporting process.

## Response

Maintainers should review concerns fairly, limit disclosure to what is needed to address them, and explain decisions to the people affected when appropriate. Depending on the circumstances, responses can include clarification, a warning, removing content, limiting participation or excluding a participant. Serious threats or persistent abuse can warrant immediate exclusion.

A person affected by a decision may request reconsideration and provide additional context through the same private route. Maintainers should avoid handling reports where they have a conflict of interest.
